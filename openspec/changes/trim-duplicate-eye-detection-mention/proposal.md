## Why

The `autofocus` écran (bloc `mise-au-point`, séance 1, slide 10) and the `designer-le-point`
écran three screens later (slide 12) each make the same claim about eye/face detection almost
word for word — "probablement la meilleure fonction de votre génération d'appareils… et beaucoup
de gens ne l'allument jamais" versus "probablement sa meilleure fonction, et beaucoup de gens ne
l'allument jamais." One screen explains autofocus modes; the other is the hands-on exercise where
the room actually sets the AF point. Saying the same thing twice inside one bloc costs projection
budget on both screens without teaching anything new the second time.

## What Changes

- Keep the eye/face-detection claim on `autofocus` (slide 10), where it belongs alongside the
  other ways of designating an AF point (single point, zone, eye/face detection).
- On `designer-le-point` (slide 12, écran `appareil-en-main`), drop the `reflex` field's repeated
  claim. The `reflex` field there currently exists only to restate that claim — remove the field
  entirely rather than leaving a half-empty aside.
